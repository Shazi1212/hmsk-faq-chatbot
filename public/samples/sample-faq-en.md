# Riverside Bike Repair: FAQ

This is a made-up example business for trying the FAQ chatbot.

## Opening hours

When are you open?
Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:30, Saturday from 10:00 to 14:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Do I need an appointment?
No appointment is needed for small repairs like flat tyres or brake adjustments. For a full service, please book a slot by phone or email so we can plan the workshop.

## Prices

How much does a basic service cost?
A basic service costs 49 euros and includes gear and brake adjustment, chain cleaning and a safety check. Parts are charged separately.

How much is a flat tyre repair?
Fixing a flat costs 15 euros including a new inner tube for standard bikes. E-bikes with hub motors in the rear wheel cost 25 euros because removing the wheel takes longer.

Do you give quotes?
Yes. For repairs above 100 euros we always call you with a quote before we start work.

## E-bikes

Do you repair e-bikes?
Yes, we service e-bikes from Bosch, Shimano and Yamaha. We can read error codes and update the motor software. We do not open or repair batteries; we send them to the manufacturer.

## Payment and pickup

Which payment methods do you accept?
Cash, debit card (girocard), credit card and PayPal. We do not accept cheques.

How long does a repair take?
Most small repairs are done while you wait or on the same day. A full service usually takes two working days. In spring (March to May) it can take up to a week.
